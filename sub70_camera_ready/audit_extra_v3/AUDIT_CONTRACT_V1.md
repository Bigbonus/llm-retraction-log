# Audit contract: the five checks in "Answers Without the Model", one row each

For each check: what you feed it, what it assumes, what it detects, what it misses, what to do when
it fires, and whether it is a validated automated detector or a human inspection. Numbers come from
the released files (`sub70_camera_ready/`); "validated" means calibrated on a known-good and a
known-bad input in those files, nothing more. Nothing here claims performance on other people's data.

| check | input | assumptions | detects | misses | after a flag | kind |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Leak check** (§2.1, App B, `paper_scripts/` not needed: the 30 lines are in the paper) | your gold-identifier generator and your distractor generator, as functions | the distractor generator is the one actually used; 400 synthetic trials per k stand in for your item set | a distractor set from which a model-free rule (position-wise majority) reconstructs the gold string; in our generator: 0/400 at k=1, 360/400 at k=3, 400/400 at k>=8; independent random ids 0/400 at every k | leakage through any other route (formatting, ordering, length); whether the model used the route; anything about generators not tested | treat the condition as solvable without the model; do not read its accuracy as capability until distractors are regenerated so that majority != gold | automated, calibrated on known-good (random ids) and known-bad (near-variants) |
| **Corpus freeze + verification** (§5, App A, App E, `paper_scripts/mythos_freeze_corpus_v1.py`, `mythos_paper_verify_v1.py`) | append-only logs with a per-row timestamp; the list of figures in the manuscript with their source rows | every reported figure is recomputable from rows; rows without a timestamp are dropped (1,146 here); a model needs >= 100 rows in a wave to be compared | figures that changed because the corpus grew or a row arrived late: 8 of 42 in draft v1, 0 of 42 after the freeze | arithmetic or logic errors shared by the manuscript and the script (it was the same author); anything not on the check list | re-derive from the frozen row set; report the drift, do not overwrite | automated; known-bad = the pre-freeze corpus (8 fail), known-good = post-freeze (0 fail) |
| **Verdict-distribution check** (§2.3) | per-row verdict labels that separate non-events (ERROR, TRUNCATED_NO_VISIBLE_OUTPUT) from wrong answers | the harness labels a row TRUNCATED only when the cap is hit and visible text is empty | a model whose low score is non-answers: 52% here vs <= 1.5% for the others | capped output that still contains text (the 4B of §2.2: 95.2% of rows hit the cap, verdict non-answer rate 1.45%, so the check was silent); any truncation the harness does not label | do not read the score as accuracy; also check the cap-hit rate (`done_reason == "length"`) before trusting a silent verdict check | automated but **not validated as a detector**: one positive, one documented miss, no held-out test |
| **Key contradiction** (§2.2) | the item text and the key, read by a person | none | a key that disagrees with the reading the text supports | anything a reader does not notice; it is not repeatable across readers in a measured way | mark the condition invalid; do not average it with valid conditions | **human inspection, no detector** |
| **Between-repeat phi** (§3, App C, `audit_extra_v3/between_repeat_phi_v3.py`) | CSV: cell, repeat, correct (0/1); non-events already excluded by you | the two repeats of a cell are the same condition; outcomes are exchangeable across repeats for the permutation p (item re-use breaks this; the file cannot check it) | between-repeat variance above binomial: injected Beta-binomial phi 2.0 reads 1.81 (p 0.001); on our 166 cells 1.076 (p 0.164); power 0.98 at phi 1.25, 0.52 at 1.10 under Beta-binomial alternatives only | excess that is not between repeats (within-repeat drift, item-by-repeat interaction, not simulated); phi below ~1.10; anything when cells sit at 0 or 1 (548 of 714 here) | do not use a binomial floor; measure the source of the excess before comparing cells | automated; smoke-tested on one known-good and one known-bad fixture, malformed input refused (12 tests); not calibrated across sample sizes or dependence forms (P1-c pending) |
| **Readable rule** (§3, `audit_extra_v3/g2_readable_flag_rate_v2.py`) | two cell sizes and two rates; phi | phi is right for your data; both cells measured the same way | a difference smaller than 2 sqrt(phi (0.25/n1 + 0.25/n2)): here it flagged 9 of 1,565 same-label repeat pairs (0.6%, shuffled 1.0%) | real differences at these sizes: for independent binomial arms 0.25 vs 0.75 it fires on 41% of draws (35% at 4–7 rows), 14% for 0.50 vs 0.80; it says "not readable", never "equal" | report the difference as within floor and stop; do not interpret direction | automated; flag rate on same-label pairs and conditional simulated power measured, no false-positive rate established |

Two things the table does not do: it does not say how often these defects occur elsewhere (one
programme), and "detects" rows are the cases we had, not a claim of coverage. The three failures
of §2 were found in the order leak (model-free solver, after the result), key (reading), verdict
(distribution); none was found by a monitor running at the time.
